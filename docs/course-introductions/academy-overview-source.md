# Welcome to Experrt Academy | Homepage and Academy overview

This is a public introduction to the learning experience at Experrt Academy. It is not an instruction to change the application, a promise of career outcomes or a lesson that earns a certificate. Use the following facts only. Do not describe the presenter as an AI trainer and do not discuss the video production tool or watermark.

## Start with the work you want to do better

Welcome to Experrt Academy. If you want to use AI more thoughtfully, make better choices about technology, understand workplace robotics or improve people processes, start with a task you recognise from your own work. You might need to check a report, write a clearer customer reply, plan a small automation or explain a change to your team. Our courses help you work through that kind of task and keep an example of what you have learned.

The catalogue includes self-paced courses in AI, technology, robotics and HR, a series on working with AI agents, and trainer-led courses for organisations. Read each outline to see who the course is for, the skills it teaches, the practical work you will produce and the account or tool access you need. You can try the Foundations sample lessons before buying an agent course.

## A learning experience you can take part in

Self-paced courses let you learn online in your own time. You work through explanations, realistic examples and questions that give feedback on your answer. The agent series also includes illustrated lessons, fictional practice files, a workbook and practical exercises. Your course progress or notes are saved to your account, so you can return to your work. Course purchase includes 12 months of access. Any software subscriptions or eligible product accounts described in the prerequisites are separate.

Written practice matters as well as reading. The existing self-paced courses provide feedback on drafts through the course tutor, while the agent series helps you keep instructions, results, checks and decisions for your practical project. Fictional examples let you try a method without sharing confidential work information. Course-specific requirements are shown on the course page.

## Assessments and work you can explain

The existing self-paced courses include lesson checks, a scenario assessment and a final practical document. You must pass the required checks, complete the document and sign your course record before receiving an Experrt certificate. That certificate has an online verification record and a downloadable PDF.

In the agent series, you submit your written project evidence for AI assessment. You receive feedback, can revise the project and earn an Experrt certificate after passing all six required skill areas. You can read the assessment report in your course, download it as a PDF and receive it by email. The assessment method and its limits are explained on the course page. An Experrt certificate is not an externally accredited qualification. Watching this overview does not earn one.

## Learning with a team

For the existing self-paced courses, organisations can buy between two and fifty places in one payment, invite learners by email and see who has joined and finished. Each person has their own learning record. Trainer-led courses are booked for a group through an enquiry and quotation. An Experrt trainer leads live sessions, in person or online, works with the group’s examples and reviews assessed work. The trainer-led course page explains the delivery and certificate requirements.

## What the skills can help you show

A finished project or practical document gives you something specific to discuss with a manager, colleague, client or prospective employer. You can explain the task, show how you checked it and describe what you would do differently next time. That is a useful starting point for a conversation about your skills and responsibilities. The Academy does not guarantee a job, promotion, higher salary, legal compliance or a fixed saving of time.
