# Agents for Developers | Course introduction

Course version: 2026.10.2-ai-assessed. This is a public introduction to this specific course, not a lesson or a promise of career outcomes.

## Who the course is for and what it helps you do

Welcome to Experrt Academy. Developers delegating clearly scoped software work and maintenance. In this course, you will learn to give an agent a small coding task, review its changes and run tests to check that the code works.

An AI agent is software that works through an agreed task using the information and tools it is allowed to access. Its actual capabilities depend on the product, account and setup you use. You remain responsible for setting limits and checking results. Do not claim that a product has been independently tested on the learner’s account.

## What the six modules cover

1. Describe the code change and how you will test it. The practical work you keep is: Issue brief and meaningful test plan.
2. Set up a separate place to work on the code. The practical work you keep is: Workspace, secret boundaries and reversal and recovery check.
3. Ask your agent to make a change you can review. The practical work you keep is: Bounded change and reasoning record.
4. Check the code, tests and any added packages. The practical work you keep is: Diff review and verified test results.
5. Fix failed checks and avoid repeating the same change. The practical work you keep is: Failure recovery and duplicate change prevention.
6. Explain your change and show that it works. The practical work you keep is: Final project pull request, handover and individual follow-up.

## How you learn and what you will produce

You study at your own pace using illustrated explanations, worked examples, practice questions with feedback and practical exercises. Your account saves your notes. Supplied fictional practice files and a workbook help you keep a record of your choices and checks. Any organisation named in the supplied practice work is fictional; no measured business results are claimed.

Your final practical project: Delegate a maintenance change, inspect its diff and tests, and demonstrate recovery without granting unattended production deployment. Keep the instructions, relevant settings, results and checks that explain what happened. Where a product step cannot be completed, label your work as a practice simulation and say what still needs testing in the actual product.

## Assessment and the certificate

Submit your written project evidence for AI assessment. You receive feedback and can revise your work. An Experrt certificate is awarded after you pass all six required skill areas. Each required area needs a score of at least three out of four. The AI assessor checks submitted text; it cannot operate an account, open external links or independently verify authorship. Your certificate records AI assessment and is not an externally accredited qualification. Watching this introduction or completing practice alone does not earn it.
