# Working with OpenAI Dots | Course introduction

Course version: 2026.10.2-ai-assessed. This is a public introduction to this specific course, not a lesson or a promise of career outcomes.

## Who the course is for and what it helps you do

Welcome to Experrt Academy. People who want to delegate ongoing research, reporting or project responsibilities in ChatGPT. In this course, you will learn to give a dot a regular task, connect the files and apps it needs, and check what it does while you are away.

An AI agent is software that works through an agreed task using the information and tools it is allowed to access. Its actual capabilities depend on the product, account and setup you use. You remain responsible for setting limits and checking results. Do not claim that a product has been independently tested on the learner’s account.

## What the six modules cover

1. Get to know your dot and where it works. The practical work you keep is: Access and environment checklist with cloud/local dependencies.
2. Give your dot a task to keep working on. The practical work you keep is: Dot instructions with checks for a correct result and stop rules.
3. Connect your apps and choose what your dot can do. The practical work you keep is: Tested app access and Custom Rules record.
4. Check the work and give your dot better information. The practical work you keep is: Annotated outputs and a corrected follow-up run.
5. Pause your dot and deal with problems. The practical work you keep is: Missing-source and conflicting-instruction drills.
6. Show how your dot helps with a real task. The practical work you keep is: Run history, source verification and project explanation.

Explain this learning journey in connected trainer language rather than reading a list of labels.

## How you learn and what you will produce

You study at your own pace using illustrated explanations, worked examples, practice questions with feedback and practical exercises. Your account saves your notes. Supplied fictional practice files and a workbook help you keep a record of your choices and checks. Any organisation named in the supplied practice work is fictional; no measured business results are claimed.

Your final practical project: Configure and evaluate a dot that maintains a project briefing, flags meaningful changes and prepares reviewable updates. Keep the instructions, relevant settings, results and checks that explain what happened. Where a product step cannot be completed, label your work as a practice simulation and say what still needs testing in the actual product.

## Assessment and the certificate

Submit your written project evidence for AI assessment. You receive feedback and can revise your work. An Experrt certificate is awarded after you pass all six required skill areas. Each required area needs a score of at least three out of four. The AI assessor checks submitted text; it cannot operate an account, open external links or independently verify authorship. Your certificate records AI assessment and is not an externally accredited qualification. Watching this introduction or completing practice alone does not earn it.
