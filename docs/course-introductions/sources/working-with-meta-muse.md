Course version: 2026.10.2-ai-assessed. This is a public introduction to this specific course, not a lesson or a promise of career outcomes.

## Who the course is for and what it helps you do

Welcome to Experrt Academy. People delegating personal planning and administrative work. In this course, you will learn to use Muse to help plan your week, manage changing priorities and ask you before making bookings or other important changes.

An AI agent is software that works through an agreed task using the information and tools it is allowed to access. Its actual capabilities depend on the product, account and setup you use. You remain responsible for setting limits and checking results. Do not claim that a product has been independently tested on the learner’s account.

## What the six modules cover

1. Set up Muse and choose something you need help with. The practical work you keep is: A clearly scoped goal and verified access checklist.
2. Tell Muse what it needs to know and remember. The practical work you keep is: List of useful information and a demonstrated correction or forgetting action.
3. Choose what Muse can do and when it must ask you. The practical work you keep is: Personal app permissions and action decision table.
4. Make a plan that fits your week. The practical work you keep is: A checked plan based on fictional constraints.
5. Update the plan when your needs change. The practical work you keep is: Changed-goal and conflicting-calendar drills.
6. Show how Muse helps you stay organised. The practical work you keep is: Plan comparison, review record and project explanation.
