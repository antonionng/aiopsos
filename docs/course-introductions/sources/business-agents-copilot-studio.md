# Business Agents with Microsoft Copilot Studio | Course introduction

Course version: 2026.10.2-ai-assessed. This is a public introduction to this specific course, not a lesson or a promise of career outcomes.

## Who the course is for and what it helps you do

Welcome to Experrt Academy. Business process owners and Microsoft ecosystem makers. In this course, you will learn to build an agent that helps sort business requests, uses the right records and asks a person when a decision needs approval.

An AI agent is software that works through an agreed task using the information and tools it is allowed to access. Its actual capabilities depend on the product, account and setup you use. You remain responsible for setting limits and checking results. Do not claim that a product has been independently tested on the learner’s account.

## What the six modules cover

1. Describe how the task works today. The practical work you keep is: Request handling map, ownership and success measures.
2. Build your agent and give it the right information. The practical work you keep is: Configured agent and example answers linked to their sources.
3. Connect tools and choose which actions need approval. The practical work you keep is: Connector tests and action permissions.
4. Choose when the task starts and who checks it. The practical work you keep is: End-to-end fictional request run.
5. Test what happens when something goes wrong. The practical work you keep is: Invalid-input, duplicate and failure tests.
6. Plan a small trial with your team. The practical work you keep is: Final project solution, reversal and recovery plan and owner handover.

## How you learn and what you will produce

You study at your own pace using illustrated explanations, worked examples, practice questions with feedback and practical exercises. Your account saves your notes. Your final practical project: Build a fictional service request handling agent that validates a request, prepares routing and escalates exceptions. Keep the instructions, relevant settings, results and checks that explain what happened.
