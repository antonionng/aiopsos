# Coordinating Multiple Agents | Course introduction

Course version: 2026.10.2-ai-assessed. This is a public introduction to this specific course, not a lesson or a promise of career outcomes.

## Who the course is for and what it helps you do

Welcome to Experrt Academy. Experienced agent users coordinating research, drafting and review responsibilities. In this course, you will learn to give each agent a clear job, check how they pass work to each other and keep a person in charge of the final result.

An AI agent is software that works through an agreed task using the information and tools it is allowed to access. Its actual capabilities depend on the product, account and setup you use. You remain responsible for setting limits and checking results. Do not claim that a product has been independently tested on the learner’s account.

## What the six modules cover

1. Decide whether you need more than one agent. The practical work you keep is: Single-agent starting measurements and justified role split.
2. Give each agent a clear job and the information it needs. The practical work you keep is: Instructions for each agent and list of records to follow.
3. Decide how agents pass work to each other. The practical work you keep is: Format for passing work between agents and rejected-input example.
4. Run the agents together and check their work. The practical work you keep is: End-to-end run with traceable responsibility.
5. Deal with disagreements and work that gets stuck. The practical work you keep is: Conflict and limits on repeated attempts drills with cost records.
6. Compare the team with using one agent. The practical work you keep is: Final project, comparative evaluation and owner handover.

## How you learn and what you will produce

You study at your own pace using illustrated explanations, worked examples, practice questions with feedback and practical exercises. Your account saves your notes. Your final practical project: Coordinate a researcher, drafter and reviewer through explicit handoffs, clearly scoped authority and an accountable final owner. Keep the instructions, relevant settings, results and checks that explain what happened. Where a product step cannot be completed, label your work as a practice simulation and say what still needs testing in the actual product.

Submit your written project evidence for AI assessment. You receive feedback and can revise your work. An Experrt certificate is awarded after you pass all six required skill areas. Each required area needs a score of at least three out of four. The AI assessor checks submitted text; it cannot operate an account, open external links or independently verify authorship. Your certificate records AI assessment and is not an externally accredited qualification. Watching this introduction or completing practice alone does not earn it.
