## Welcome and the problem this course helps you solve

Welcome to Experrt Academy. Always-On Agents: Foundations is for people who want help with tasks they do regularly at work or in their business, and want to understand how to check that help. An AI agent is software that can follow agreed instructions, use the information and tools it is allowed to access, and work through a task. Some agents can continue agreed work between conversations. What an agent can actually do depends on the product, account and setup you use.

Think about preparing a weekly project report. You may need to find the latest records, write an update, check the facts and explain what needs attention. This course helps you plan that work clearly before asking an agent to help. You stay responsible for deciding what the agent can do and checking its results.

## What you will learn

The course has six modules. You will choose a task you do every day or week, and record how long it takes today. You will write clear instructions, including what a correct result should look like. You will choose the information the agent can see and the actions it is allowed to take.

Next, you will plan when the task should run and practise checking a result against its source records. You will work through a problem, check for repeated work and keep a record of costs. Finally, you will bring those pieces together and explain how another person could run and check your task.

## What the learning experience includes

You learn at your own pace through illustrated explanations, worked examples, practice questions with feedback and practical exercises. Your notes are saved to your account. Fictional practice files and a workbook help you try the method without using confidential business information. Northstar Studio is a fictional design business supplied for practice. You can begin with basic file and browser skills. Where a product step cannot be completed in your account, you can document a practice simulation and say what still needs testing in the real tool.

## What you will produce and how it is assessed

Your final practical project is a task that prepares a weekly project report. You will keep your instructions, checked results, a record of a problem and its correction, a cost worksheet and an explanation another person can follow.

You submit your written evidence to the AI assessor. It checks the six required skill areas and gives feedback. You can revise work that needs improvement. Passing every required skill area earns an Experrt certificate. The certificate records AI assessment and is not an externally accredited qualification. The assessor checks submitted text; it cannot operate your account, open outside links or independently verify authorship.

## How this can help you at work

These skills can help you explain a task more clearly, judge whether an agent's result is ready to use and show a colleague how you checked it. Your project gives you a concrete example to discuss with a manager, client or prospective employer. The value comes from being able to demonstrate your choices and checks, rather than simply saying that you use AI. The course does not guarantee a job, promotion, pay increase or a particular amount of time saved.
