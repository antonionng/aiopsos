## Measurement and the next editorial decisions

1. In the verified Google Search Console property, submit https://www.experrt.com/sitemap.xml and inspect the insights index, HR subject page and new articles. Search Console access and submission have not been performed in this task.
2. After sufficient search data accumulates, compare impressions, query relevance, clicks and click-through rate for each landing page. Avoid interpreting small samples as a stable result.
3. Review enquiries carrying source_article alongside their actual suitability for training or platform work. Existing article footer CTAs pass the article slug to the contact form. Search attribution is not the same as a complete conversion analytics implementation.
4. Improve pages that attract relevant impressions but answer the reader's question poorly. Do not create near-duplicate pages for slight keyword variations.
5. Commission first-hand examples from real delivery with permission before publishing customer outcomes, testimonials or quantified savings.
