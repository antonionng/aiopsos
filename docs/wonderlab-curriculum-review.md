# Wonderlab curriculum review matrix

Content release: 2026-10-07.1. Prepared 7 October 2026.

All 24 definitions have a stated outcome, four activities, a final challenge, explanatory feedback and a project with checks. The outcome, final challenge and project descriptions below were inspected during the technical review. This confirms coverage, not demonstrated learning, reading-age suitability or value. A human educational reviewer and family pilot must assess the depth and suitability.

Review each complete mission in the player, including wrong answers, hints, narration, creation tools and downloads. Use the family protocol in [the launch review](wonderlab-launch-review.md). Record a dated result and a specific revision if a learner can guess the correct button without understanding the skill.

## Review criteria

- The learner can explain what to do and why it relates to AI. Fixed instructions and learning from examples must remain distinct.
- A wrong attempt receives an explanation that helps the learner repair it. A fresh final challenge changes the situation, rather than merely repeating an answer.
- A project gives the learner something to make, test and improve. Self-ticked checks and selected answers are useful practice evidence but do not establish independent competence.
- Younger learners can participate with the intended adult support. Older learners have enough meaningful creation and testing for the stated duration.
- Instructions, examples and feedback work without audio. Touch, keyboard and reduced-motion options remain usable.

The final challenges in several older missions ask the learner to choose a proposed action. Reviewers should pay particular attention to whether the associated practical project actually demonstrates the stated skill, such as writing and checking a brief, rather than recognising the longest or most careful-sounding option.

## Evidence to add after review

For each row, record reviewer/date, content version, anonymous pilot session references, what the learner could demonstrate, adult assistance required, observed timing, identified issue, revision and retest result. Keep child names and private creations out of this repository. No row should be changed to passed solely because automated answer-key tests pass.
